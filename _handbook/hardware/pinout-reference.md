## Board identity

Record the exact board name, hardware revision, datasheet link, and date checked. Pin assignments can differ between board revisions.

## Checks before wiring

Confirm logic voltage, boot-strapping behavior, peripheral pin-sharing restrictions, and connector orientation from the board documentation. Verify wiring against the exact board revision before powering the circuit.
